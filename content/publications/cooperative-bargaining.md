## Overview

How can an edge service provider and self-interested mobile devices cooperate in a way that is both efficient and fair? This work formulates their interaction as a **Nash bargaining problem**, jointly considering task dispatch, computing-power allocation, and reward distribution.

## Approach

The key structure separates efficiency from fairness: task dispatch and resource allocation determine the total cooperation surplus, while rewards determine how that surplus is shared.

The problem is decomposed into social-welfare maximization and optimal reward distribution. A distributed ADMM algorithm and a negotiation algorithm solve the respective subproblems with limited exchange of private parameters.

## Evaluation

The study uses Google ClusterData 2019 and Shanghai Telecom mobility traces, including experiments on efficiency, fairness, scalability, and sensitivity.
