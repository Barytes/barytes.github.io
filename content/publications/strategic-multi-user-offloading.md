## Overview

This manuscript extends the study of strategic user offloading and independent service-provider pricing in mobile edge computing. It models a two-stage multi-leader, multi-follower Stackelberg game and characterizes the users’ interaction as an exact generalized potential game.

## Approach

The work studies equilibrium existence and the computational difficulty of the follower problem. It develops distributed primal-dual resource allocation and an offloading-user selection algorithm with a theoretical approximation bound.

At the provider stage, the analysis investigates **switching prices**: boundaries at which the equilibrium set of offloading users changes. This structure supports best-response estimation and an iterative pricing algorithm.
