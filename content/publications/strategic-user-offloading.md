## Overview

Mobile edge computing brings computation closer to users, but the participants do not necessarily share the same objectives. Users decide whether and how to offload their tasks, while computation and network service providers choose prices to maximize their own revenues.

This paper models these interactions as a two-stage, multi-leader, multi-follower **Stackelberg game**. It studies the users’ generalized Nash equilibrium and the service providers’ pricing equilibrium.

## Approach

The framework combines a greedy algorithm for user offloading, an incremental improvement algorithm for provider pricing, and a branch-and-bound procedure for equilibrium computation. Numerical simulations compare the resulting solutions with oracle and baseline approaches.
