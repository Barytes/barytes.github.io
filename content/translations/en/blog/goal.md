---
title: "Goals"
description: "Simplify life into four areas—work, learning, health, and relationships—and set short-term goals and actions for each, turning decisions into daily habits."
---

How can I live each day with more happiness and order? I think the answer is to balance the different parts of life and create meaning in each of them. The best-known source of this idea is Paul J. Meyer's Wheel of Life, which divides life into 8–12 sectors, such as work, finances, exercise and health, **relationships**, personal growth, spirituality and faith, and hobbies. It gives life a measure of resilience: meaning does not depend on a single pillar. Even when one area becomes difficult, I can find meaning by working to improve several others. It also reminds me not to neglect any one area too much, because doing so can substantially diminish my experience of life.

I find eight areas hard to keep in mind. I would simplify them into four: work (work + finances), learning (personal growth + hobbies), health (body and mind), and relationships (connections with other people). For each area, I set a short-term goal and decide what actions to take. I write down those decisions and turn them into daily habits, reducing the pressure of making decisions every day and helping me follow through.
