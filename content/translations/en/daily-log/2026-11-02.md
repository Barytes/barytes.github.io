- Thought experiment: Is recursive self-improvement (RSI) a kind of network effect for AI?
  - More capable AI could build more and stronger AI, improving both model capabilities and infrastructure.
  - Network effects arise when more participants joining creates more value. Does the value of intelligence also grow when other intelligences join or are created?
  - My tentative view is that this requires an extra condition. I don't yet know what's missing.
  - DeepSeek's take: If different intelligences are substitutes by default, the result will be concentration, with no network effect.
  - For the marginal value of a new participant to exceed its marginal cost, some mechanism has to make communication, coordination, verification, and the division of authority and responsibility cheap enough. I haven't seen such a mechanism in AI yet.
  - Firms can't keep hiring indefinitely either. New hires may duplicate skills the team already has, reducing their marginal value. More layers of management can raise marginal costs through information loss and distorted incentives.
- Notes from 课代表立正's podcast on fake work:
  - Fake work arises because the usefulness and productivity of knowledge work are hard to measure.
  - If you work for someone else, be clear-eyed about it: fake work is what you're getting paid for. Do it well and serve your client or boss.
  - Two ways out of fake work: do 10% more high-value work and crowd it out, or go out on your own and face the market directly.
