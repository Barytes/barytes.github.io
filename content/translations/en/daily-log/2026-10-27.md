### Agents for Real-Time Understanding of the Real World

- Aero Realtime: [paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.08469) (1 hour)
  - Overview: a 4B streaming multimodal model with a duplex architecture.
  - Problem: How can we train multimodal models capable of real-time interaction?
  - Previous work: (1) Turn-based interaction, with prefill followed by decoding in each turn; new input cannot be received during decoding. (2) Splitting the input stream into micro-turns. (3) Using a control module outside the model, with response timing handled by a decision mechanism separate from word generation, rather than unified as next-token prediction in the same output stream.
  - Insight: Within each 80 ms micro-turn/slot, add the audio/video input and the model's output from the previous slot into a single representation, then feed it back into the model. The output can be silence or a language token.
  - Technical overview: (1) Inference produces one token per 80 ms slot, reusing the KV cache. (2) Training distributes input video frames across multiple GPUs with load balancing, packs speech and text samples into compact long sequences, and shares their processing across GPUs.
- DIVE: [paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28405) (0.5 hours)
  - Overview: a benchmark for full-duplex audio-video interaction.
  - Problem: How can we evaluate multimodal models' full-duplex interaction capabilities?
  - Previous work: (1) Following an offline evaluation paradigm. (2) Existing online and streaming benchmarks primarily assess causal multimodal perception through timestamped question answering—answering questions at specific points in time without looking at future information.
  - Insight: Evaluate three capabilities: (1) Proactive intervention—intervening at an appropriate time based on cues. (2) Sustained engagement—continuously answering questions or taking notes. (3) Interactive collaboration—collaborating during overlapping interactions involving multiple parties, including interruptions and conversations with multiple speakers.
  - Technical overview: 525 instances across six task categories.
