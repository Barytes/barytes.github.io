- Read Lectures 1 and 2 of Stanford CS197 (2 hours).
  - Scientific discovery: propose a new idea that differs from previous assumptions, and demonstrate that it is the best idea for the problem.
  - How to read a paper: grasp the big idea; you do not need to understand everything. Specifically, explain the problem it addresses, previous work's assumptions, the paper's insight and its implementation, how the insight is shown to be effective, and its impact on the problem.
  - Research Log: writing a Research Log helps track progress toward research goals and communicate with collaborators.
  - Literature review: the aim is to find the bit flip—how long-standing a hidden assumption your paper overturns—including both an individual paper's bit flip and the literature's bit flip.
