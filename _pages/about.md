I’m **Beiyan Liu (刘倍延)**, an MPhil student (Software Engineering) at **Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU)**.

## Research
My current work is around **Mobile Edge Computing (MEC)** and incentive design:

- **Strategic user offloading & service provider pricing** (Stackelberg game among users / providers).  
  This line of work extends an ICASSP 2026 accepted paper toward an IEEE TMC submission.

- **Efficient and fair computing power provision** via **Nash Bargaining Solution (NBS)** style incentives.

## Interests
